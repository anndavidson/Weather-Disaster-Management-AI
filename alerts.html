🧠 How the Risk Engine Works

  1. The user selects a location.
  2. The system retrieves the weather forecast dataset.
  3. The system reads rainfall, wind, pressure, soil moisture, runoff, temperature and atmospheric data.
  4. Each hazard is analysed separately.
  5. A project-defined risk score from 0 to 100 is generated.
  6. The score is converted into Low, Moderate, High or Severe.

⚠️ Important

These are weather-based risk indicators created for this academic project. They are not official government disaster warnings.

Actual flood, cyclone and disaster warnings require additional information such as river levels, terrain, drainage conditions, storm structure, satellite observations and official meteorological analysis.
